## Find an existing walk

1. Open [bitcoinwalk.org](https://bitcoinwalk.org/).
2. Search by city, alternative city name, address or nearby place.
3. Open the city card to see its next published walk.
4. Check the local date, time and meeting point before travelling.

Only upcoming walks appear in the main discovery grid. A city can still have a public page when its next date has not yet been scheduled.

## Start one in your city

Choose **Start a BitcoinWalk** and complete the guided form. You will:

1. select or create a Nostr identity;
2. choose the city and confirm the meeting pin;
3. provide a public description, date and meeting point;
4. choose whether the walk repeats; and
5. review and sign the submission.

Weekly and repeating plans initially prepare a series of eight walks. Nothing is published without the required signatures and approval.

## Basic and Pro cities

Basic cities use shared BitcoinWalk infrastructure. Pro cities add managed city identity, payment and relay capabilities after a verified payment and host setup. Selecting Pro does not bypass review or transfer control of the host's identity.
